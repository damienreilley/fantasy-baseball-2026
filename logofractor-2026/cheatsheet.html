Opening cheat sheet

Four checks. No hunting through explanations.

🖨️ Open printable PDF⬇️ Save cheat-sheet image
1
Check the shine

Repeating MLB logos? That is the LogoFractor finish. Without the pattern, it may be ordinary Chrome.

2
Turn it over

Stamp such as 012/150 = numbered parallel. Check the color ladder.

3
Read the code

1–300 base/photo variation. FS Future Stars. P Perspectives. BTP Big Ticket. WC Wrecking Crew. 91CB 1991 Topps. RA autograph checklist.

4
Set aside for checking

Real autograph · numbered card · different photo · RC rookie.
