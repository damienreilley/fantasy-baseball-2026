④ THE COLOR CHEAT SHEET

Color is the parallel. Flavor is the card family.

Important: A purple Grand Slam Grape design is normal for the Grape insert family. That does not automatically mean it is a Purple parallel. Check the serial stamp and special foil/color treatment.

The print-run ladder is documented for the three flavor inserts. Other families have different parallels: do not apply flavor print runs to regular base, Game Face, Crew, or autographs. A code alone does not identify its color.
DON'T MISS A HIT

Cards to stop and examine

🔢 Numbered cards

Pulse states six numbered cards per Blaster box. Check fronts and backs for stamps like 035/399.

✍ Autographs

Big League Chew Signatures and Shredded Signatures. A real signature can be on a sticker or card, not merely printed as art.

🎭 Game Face

Stylized cartoon player illustrations. Pulse lists Game Face as an ultra-rare case-hit family.

🧢 Big League Chew Crew

Brand characters (Blaze Bailey, Hook Hiro, etc.). Pulse also lists these as case hits.
