DON'T MISS THESE

⚠️ Stop and check these before putting cards away

✍ AUTOGRAPHA real ink signature or a signed sticker; a printed signature in a photo does not count.
🧩 RELIC / PATCHActual material embedded in the card. Look for a cutout, thick stock or material window.
🔢 NUMBERED CARDA stamped “09/25” or “01/01” on front/back. Check EVERY card, including inserts.
🪞 GOLDEN MIRROR / PHOTO VARIATIONOften a different player photograph or special design — may NOT have a serial number.
🎟 REDEMPTIONScratch-off code or text promising a card or prize. Do not throw away. 75 Years Gifts details ↗
⭐ ROOKIE / RCThe RC logo identifies an official rookie card; it's not automatically a special parallel.
🏟 PHILLY ALL-STAR FOILRepeating diagonal 2026 All-Star logos. This is an exclusive Series 2 parallel, not a fourth base set.
🏆 RARE INSERTHome Field Advantage, Heavy Lumber, 1991 Topps, other numbered or uncommon inserts. Card codes often have letters.
